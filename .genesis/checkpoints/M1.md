## Scope decision · 2026-07-30 (user-approved, before iter 1)

The worker ships in M1 as a **separate compose service running a no-op heartbeat loop** (same image as
`api`, different command), and `GET /v1/health` reports its heartbeat freshness from Redis. Rationale: the
invariant `background_jobs_never_block_the_request_path` becomes structurally true at the first milestone
instead of being retrofitted in M5, and `tests/invariants/test_api_worker_separation.py` starts guarding
`app/api/** → worker/**` imports immediately. M5 extends that test rather than introducing it.
`PLAN.md` M1 and `DONE.html` §3 were updated to match, with the user's approval.
